Exotic dance moves

You were right — during my post-doctorate work, I did spend time studying exotic tribal dances. Below are a few favourites, along with short explanations.

Polynesian chicken dance
A surprisingly influential dance dating back to around 300 BCE, traditionally performed to encourage prosperity and growth.
Icelandic brownian shuffle
A dance used for warmth before fire became common, involving close group movement and very small, rapid motions. Some believe it helped inspire modern styles like Twerking.
Arctic robot dance
A movement style mistakenly identified as a dance by explorers in the 1960s, later understood to simply be people reacting to extreme cold.
